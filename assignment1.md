1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW 
This report presents the first phase of the marketing project for the food truck Safar-e-Zaiqa, a desi food 
startup focused on biryani and pulao. The concept is built on real market insights collected through 20 
field interviews conducted around university areas and nearby hotspots. 
Unlike generic fast food brands, Safar-e-Zaiqa focuses on affordable, hygienic, and authentic desi 
meals, targeting students who seek taste, value, and trust. The brand emphasizes a journey of flavors 
(Safar) — delivering food straight from the daig to the heart. 
 
2. RESEARCH SUMMARY (1000–1200 WORDS) 
The purpose of this research was to evaluate the feasibility of launching a biryani & pulao-focused 
food truck near university areas. A structured survey and interviews were conducted with 20 
respondents, including students, employees, and local residents. The research focused on food 
preferences, price sensitivity, hygiene concerns, and expectations from food trucks. 
Demographic Insights 
The majority of respondents (around 70%) belonged to the 18–25 age group, confirming that students 
are the dominant market segment. A smaller portion included employees and families. This indicates 
that the food truck should primarily target young consumers with limited budgets but high food 
demand. 
Why Biryani & Pulao? (Key Research Insight) 
Unlike the original fast-food concept, the research revealed a strong gap in the local market: 
• Two nearby competitors:

• Problem identified:  
o Prices are too high for students  
o Portion sizes are not satisfying

Students clearly expressed the need for: 
• Affordable desi food

strong emotional and cultural connection. 
Safar-e-Zaiqa is not just a food truck —
